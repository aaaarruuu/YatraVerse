## Overview
Mandu is a hilltop fort town on the Vindhya plateau in the Dhar district of Madhya Pradesh. It is known for the Afghan-style architecture of the Malwa Sultanate, with palaces, tombs and mosques spread across the plateau.

## History
Mandu became the capital of the Malwa Sultanate under Hoshang Shah in the 15th century. The Mughal emperor Akbar took the fort in 1561, after the defeat of Baz Bahadur. The love story of Baz Bahadur and Rani Roopmati is a well-known local legend.

## Main monuments
The Jahaz Mahal, or Ship Palace, stands between two artificial lakes and looks like a ship. The Hindola Mahal is a palace with sloping walls. Other important sites include the marble tomb of Hoshang Shah, the Jami Masjid, and the palaces linked with Baz Bahadur and Rani Roopmati.

## Visiting hours
The monuments can be visited during the daytime. Check the official Archaeological Survey of India and Madhya Pradesh Tourism websites for current timings and ticket prices before you travel.

## Best time to visit
The best time to visit is from October to March, when the weather is pleasant. The monsoon from July to September turns the plateau green and is also a popular time to visit. Summers are hot.

## Getting there
Mandu is about 35 km from the town of Dhar. Indore, about 95 km away by road, has the nearest major airport and railway station. Buses and taxis run from Indore and Dhar.
