## Overview
The Taj Mahal is a white marble mausoleum on the bank of the Yamuna river in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. It was commissioned by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, who died in 1631. It is one of the best-known examples of Mughal architecture and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983.

## History and construction
Construction began around 1632. The main mausoleum was completed around 1643, and the rest of the complex (mosque, guest house, gardens and gateway) was finished by about 1653. Ustad Ahmad Lahori is traditionally credited as the chief architect. The white marble came from Makrana in Rajasthan, and thousands of artisans and workers took part in the building.

## Architecture
The mausoleum stands on a raised platform with four minarets at the corners and a large central dome. The walls are decorated with pietra dura inlay of semi-precious stones and with calligraphy of Quranic verses. The complex follows a charbagh (four-part) garden layout with a central water channel, and the tomb is perfectly symmetrical.

## Visiting hours and closing day
The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays for visitors, because the mosque in the complex is used for Friday prayers by worshippers. It is open on the other days from sunrise to sunset. Night viewing is offered on a few nights around the full moon each month, and it is not held on Fridays or during Ramadan. Check the official Archaeological Survey of India website for current timings and ticket prices before you travel.

## Best time to visit
The best time to visit Agra is from October to March, when the weather is cool and clear. Summers from April to June are very hot, and the monsoon months of July to September bring humidity and clouds. Sunrise is the least crowded time and the marble changes colour in the early light. Winter mornings can have fog that hides the view.

## Getting there
Agra is about 200 km from Delhi. Trains from Delhi reach Agra in a few hours, and the Yamuna Expressway connects the two cities by road. The nearest airport is in Agra, and Delhi is the main international gateway.
